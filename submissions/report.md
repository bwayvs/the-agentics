
# CrewAI Multi-Agent Workflow Reflection

## Topic
AI in Building Energy Efficiency

## Changes Made
I modified the original CrewAI workflow by adding a fourth agent called Fact Checker. The original workflow included a Research Analyst, Technical Writer, and Editor. My updated workflow uses four agents that work together sequentially.

I also changed the research topic to AI in Building Energy Efficiency, which relates to my EcoBuild capstone project.

## Results
The workflow generated a research brief, reviewed the claims for accuracy, wrote an explanation, and edited the final output. The Fact Checker identified several statistics and claims that needed additional sources.

## Challenges
I encountered errors when the Technical Writer attempted to generate responses. I also encountered an API rate-limit error. The workflow eventually completed successfully after retrying.

## What I Learned
This assignment helped me understand how multiple AI agents can work together on different parts of a task. I found the Fact Checker especially useful because it identified claims that should not be accepted without supporting evidence.

I can see how this approach could be useful in my EcoBuild project for researching energy efficiency, reviewing model findings, and preparing explanations for users.
